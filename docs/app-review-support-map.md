# Echo App Review Support Map

Reviewers can access all support, privacy, and legal surfaces directly from `Settings`.

1. Open `Settings > Support > Help & FAQ` for the searchable support hub and quick actions.
2. Open `Settings > Support > Contact Support`, `Report a Problem`, or `Send Feedback` to start an email-based support flow.
3. Open `Settings > About > Privacy Policy` and `Terms of Use` to review legal documents in-app.
4. Open `Settings > About > Data & AI Usage` for the in-app explanation of permissions and feature-related data usage.
5. Open `Settings > About > Third-Party Acknowledgements` to review external libraries used by the app.

Additional entry points:

- `Profile > Privacy & Security` opens the same `Data & AI Usage` screen.
- The authentication and premium screens also expose working `Terms` and `Privacy` links.
